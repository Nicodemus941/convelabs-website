# ConveLabs Command Center

Purpose: give ConveLabs one operating sheet for local-market saturation, with Winter Park as the beachhead.

This document is not a brand essay. It is the control panel.

## 1. Objective

Primary goal:
- Saturate Winter Park so ConveLabs becomes the obvious local choice for concierge mobile phlebotomy.

Business goal:
- Increase qualified bookings, repeat visits, reviews, and physician/practice referrals.

Positioning rule:
- ConveLabs wins first as a premium local service business, not as a generic software company.

## 2. Success Metrics

Track these weekly:
- Bookings from Winter Park zip codes: `32789`, `32792`
- Revenue from Winter Park bookings
- Cost per booked appointment by channel
- Lead-to-booking conversion rate
- Booking-start to checkout conversion rate
- Google Business Profile calls, direction requests, website clicks
- Google review count and average rating
- Count of reviews mentioning `Winter Park`
- Number of active Winter Park-area referral partners
- Return-patient rate

Minimum dashboard sources:
- Website analytics
- Booking funnel analytics
- Google Business Profile insights
- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- CRM / appointment system

## 4. Standing Authority Needed

If control is real, approval cannot bottleneck execution.

Recommended standing approvals:
- Launch and pause ads within a monthly budget cap
- Publish landing pages and location pages
- Update website copy and CTAs
- Reply to Google reviews
- Post on social channels
- Send outbound outreach to approved partner categories
- Create and test offers
- Run remarketing campaigns

Recommended guardrails:
- No public medical claims outside approved language
- No promises around insurance coverage unless confirmed
- No pricing changes above a set threshold without approval
- No press statements or legal/compliance responses without approval

## 9. Non-Negotiables

- Fast lead response matters as much as traffic volume.
- Winter Park saturation is not just ads; it is search, reviews, referrals, and repetition.
- If booking ops break, growth spend gets wasted.
- If review generation is weak, local ranking gets capped.
- If partner outreach is inconsistent, ConveLabs stays dependent on direct-response spend.
